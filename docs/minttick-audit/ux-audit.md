# MintTick — UX, Trading UX, and Engineering Audit

Format: **Issue ID | Screen | Component/File | Current Behavior | Problem | User Impact | Severity | Proposed Fix | Validation Method**

Severity: Critical (could mislead a trading decision or customer safety), High (blocks task or erodes trust), Medium (friction or inconsistency), Low (polish).

Screen references (`S-…`) map to `screen-by-screen-review.md`.
