# PAPER automation customer story

As a signed-in customer, I can keep my account in PAPER mode and choose Manual or Automated execution. In Manual I decide when to submit a PAPER trade. In Automated I explicitly allow eligible AI opportunities to initiate PAPER trades for me. In both cases the same server risk controls protect buying power, per-trade risk, stop loss, target and emergency stop.

I can see what the system is doing in normal trading language: watching opportunities, checking risk, accepted, filled, position open, P&L and closed. I can pause trading, return to Manual, and see portfolio/history state reconstructed from the backend.

I cannot enable LIVE trading or broker submission in this release. Connecting Angel One gives read-only account/broker data and does not change that boundary.
