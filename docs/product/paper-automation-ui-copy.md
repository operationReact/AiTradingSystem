# PAPER automation customer UI copy

Use these concepts in the primary mobile experience. Exact typography/layout remains governed by the canonical Figma design system.

## Global controls

- Trading mode: **PAPER**
- LIVE affordance: **Live trading — Coming later**
- Execution: **Manual** / **Automated**
- Safety state: **Risk protected**
- Emergency state: **Trading paused**

## Automated PAPER states

- **AI opportunity found**
- **Checking risk**
- **PAPER order accepted**
- **Filled**
- **Position open**
- **Target reached**
- **Stop loss reached**
- **Closed**
- **Profit** / **Loss**

## Rejections

Use direct customer language, for example:

- “This trade exceeds your configured risk limit.”
- “Trading is paused by your safety controls.”
- “AI confidence is below your automation threshold.”
- “This trade is larger than your available PAPER buying power.”

Do not display raw backend enums, exception names, provider payloads, reconciliation terminology, access keys, internal audit vocabulary or authorization status codes in normal customer flows.
