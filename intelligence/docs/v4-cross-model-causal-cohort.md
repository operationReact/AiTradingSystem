# V4-04 — Canonical cross-family causal cohort and cost binding

## Why this correction is necessary

PRs #362 (bounded patch transformer), #363 (opt-in Chronos-2 interface),
#364 (bounded TimeMixer-style), and #365 (bounded TFT-style) used slightly
different JSON field names for their paired **fold** and **cost** fingerprints.
For example, one called the holdout observations `cohort`, another
`members`, and a third `observations`; round-trip costs were variously
named `cost_bps`, `round_trip_cost_bps`, or
`assumed_round_trip_cost_bps`.

Those comparisons were internally matched for each *single* challenger but
the existing V4-03 cross-model `TournamentCandidate` checker requires
identical `fold_binding_fingerprint`,
`economic_cost_binding_fingerprint`, and exact decision-level membership.
Thus results from the very same NSE/BSE holdout could not be pooled into a
registered cross-family tournament.

## New contract

`india-v4-cross-model-causal-cohort.v1` is the immutable, fingerprinted
contract generated by `bind_common_causal_tournament_cohort(parent, rows,
target)`. The caller must supply the already validated PR #361 causal
baseline report and exact holdout rows. It rejects an INCONCLUSIVE parent,
wrong target, changed or reordered observations, altered row count,
different trading sessions, and any tampered content. It fixes:

- Same training/holdout export, model target, fee-book provenance hashes
- Same ordered decision times and observation hashes
- A single **canonical** fold hash over training/holdout export plus exact
  causal holdout membership
- A single **canonical** cost hash over the India fee-book and target,
  including the assumed round-trip cost inside the target fingerprint
- A portable `series_kwargs()` mapping accepted by the existing
  `create_net_return_series` helper and `TournamentCandidate` logic

All four V4-04 challengers now use the shared constructor for **both**
baseline and challenger series; a change to the market source, lookback
cohort, target or fee schedule cannot silently pass as the same trial.
This is a compatibility and evidence-integrity fix, not a recalculation of
published model performance, a new standalone model, or a production API.

## Remaining acceptance

The neural research reports contain aggregate metrics and hashes but do
not expose the underlying individual per-decision predictions and after-
cost net-return series as separately auditable immutable artifacts.
Therefore this PR **does not claim to have run a four-family tournament**.
The next implementation must emit and independently verify each candidate's
actual ordered `ModelPredictionSeries` and `NetReturnSeries`, bind to its
registered model/version/run and to PR #360's prospective trial budget,
and only then call the familywise-corrected `audit_registered_trials`.

The earlier PR #359 holdout freeze and PR #360 trial-budget manifest must be
created and timestamped **before** collecting the independent market cohort.
Even a complete self-declared trial audit cannot prove market-data
authenticity, hidden attempt exclusion or real out-of-sample profitability.

Every output remains research only: no independent provenance or trusted
external pre-registration, no promotion/capital authority, and no
PAPER/SHADOW/LIVE acceptance. No new service secrets, broker orders or
production deployment are involved.
